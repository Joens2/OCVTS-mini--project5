Description:

Palawan is a long, beautiful island province in the Philippines, celebrated for its limestone cliffs, clear lagoons, and rich marine life. In El Nido, boat trips explore sheltered bays and dramatic rock formations, while nearby Coron is known for scenic lakes and excellent snorkeling and diving. The Puerto Princesa Underground River winds through a limestone cave and is part of a protected national park. Visitors can also find quiet beaches, fishing villages, and coral reefs throughout the region. Palawan's natural beauty makes it a wonderful destination for kayaking, swimming, wildlife watching, or simply enjoying the warm tropical scenery. Travelers are encouraged to respect local communities and help protect the delicate coastal environment.

Fun Things to do!

  • Island-hop around El Nido: Join a permitted boat tour to visit lagoons, beaches, and limestone cliffs. Check what gear and fees are included.
  • Kayak in a lagoon: Paddle at a relaxed pace and take in the dramatic rock scenery while following local rules to protect the water and wildlife.
  • Explore the Puerto Princesa Underground River: Take a guided boat trip through this remarkable cave system; reserve ahead because visitor numbers are managed.
  • Snorkel or dive in Coron: Discover coral reefs and marine life with a reputable local operator, and avoid touching coral or wildlife.

Fun Facts

  • The Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
  • Palawan is an archipelago province made up of many islands and islets, not just one island.
  • Tubbataha Reefs Natural Park, in the Sulu Sea, is another UNESCO World Heritage Site in Palawan province.
