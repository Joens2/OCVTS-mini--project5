Description:

Bora Bora is a small island in French Polynesia, famous for its turquoise lagoon and the green volcanic peak of Mount Otemanu. A barrier reef surrounds much of the island, creating calm waters for boat tours, swimming, snorkeling, and diving among colorful marine life. Visitors can explore the main island by road, relax on white-sand beaches, or learn about Polynesian history and traditions in local communities. The lagoon's changing shades of blue and the dramatic mountain views make Bora Bora especially popular for a peaceful tropical escape. As with any island destination, thoughtful travel helps protect the reefs and supports the people who call the island home.

Fun Things to do!

  • Take a lagoon tour: See Bora Bora's vivid blue water and reef from a boat, with stops chosen by a local guide.
  • Snorkel in the lagoon: Look for colorful fish and coral in calm areas, while keeping a respectful distance from marine life.
  • Visit Matira Beach: Spend a relaxing day swimming or watching the sunset along one of the island's best-known public beaches.
  • See Mount Otemanu: Enjoy views of the island's dramatic volcanic peak from a boat or a guided land excursion; access to some trails may be restricted.

Fun Facts

  • Mount Otemanu is the remnant of an extinct volcano and rises about 727 meters (2,385 feet) above the lagoon.
  • A coral reef surrounds Bora Bora's lagoon, with small coral islets called motus along the reef.
  • Bora Bora is part of the Society Islands in French Polynesia, a French overseas collectivity in the South Pacific.
