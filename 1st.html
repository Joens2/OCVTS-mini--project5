Description:

Maui is one of Hawaii's most beloved islands, known for its striking mix of volcanic landscapes, lush valleys, and beautiful coastlines. Visitors can drive the winding Road to Hana past waterfalls, rainforests, and ocean viewpoints, or watch the sunrise above the clouds at Haleakala National Park. Along the shore, beaches offer places to swim, snorkel, relax, and look for seasonal visits from humpback whales. The island also has welcoming towns, local farms, and a rich Hawaiian culture that visitors can experience through food, music, and community traditions. Whether you are seeking outdoor adventures or a peaceful place to unwind, Maui offers a memorable blend of nature and island hospitality.

Fun Things to do!

  • Drive the Road to Hana: Take a scenic drive past rainforest, waterfalls, and ocean viewpoints. Plan plenty of time for stops and check road conditions before you go.
  • Visit Haleakala National Park: Explore the volcanic landscape and enjoy sweeping views. Sunrise visits require advance planning and reservations.
  • Snorkel at the coast: See tropical fish and coral in clear waters; choose a safe, permitted beach and follow local ocean-safety guidance.
  • Explore a local town: Browse shops, try regional food, and learn about Maui's culture and community.

Fun Facts

  • Haleakala's summit is about 10,023 feet (3,055 meters) above sea level.
  • Maui is nicknamed the “Valley Isle,” after the broad valley between its two major volcanic regions.
  • Humpback whales visit Hawaiian waters in winter, and the waters around Maui are a popular place to spot them.
